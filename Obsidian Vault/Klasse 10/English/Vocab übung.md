How does Rebecca try to appeal to peoples hearts?

The article published b Rebecca Hosking describes how filming wildlife on Midway Island exposed her to the devastating impact of plastic pollution, especially on albatrosses that ingest plastic and die. Shocked by the scale of the problem, she realized how unaware she had been. After returning home, she took action by sharing her documentary and raising awareness in her community. This led to Modbury becoming the first town in Europe to ban plastic bags. The campaign spread internationally and proved successful without harming trade. Hosking concludes by stressing the long-lasting environmental damage caused by plastic and criticizes society’s throwaway culture.

facts:
- 80% of marine litter comes from land
- 90% of that litter is plastic
- Ocean currents concentrate rubbish in large areas
- Plastic carries toxins that enter the food chain 
- These toxins can cause cancer 
- Plastic takes about 400 years (or more) to degrade 
- It takes 300 million years to form the oil used to make plastic
- Oceans cover seven-tenths of the Earth
- Plastic pollution is increasing rapidly
- Modbury traders saved around half a million plastic bags

how rebecca appeal
- Uses strong emotional imagery and personal experience
- Describes dead birds and chicks surrounded by plastic
- Shows animals suffering or dying after ingesting waste
- Uses vivid, shocking language (e.g. “apocalypse”, “haunted me”)
- Conveys urgency and seriousness of the problem
- Shares her own emotions (crying, feeling helpless) to build empathy
- Highlights innocent wildlife to evoke sympathy
- Links pollution to human health
- Encourages responsibility and guilt about plastic use
